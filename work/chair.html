03 — The Problem

Bus stops, queues, platforms and corridors are full of people waiting on their feet. A bench takes space these places simply don't have.

How might we

…give people somewhere to rest for a few minutes, in less space than a seat?

01

Long waits standing

Even short waits tire legs and backs, especially for older people.

02

No room for seating

Narrow platforms and corridors can't fit benches.

03

Hard to get up

Low seats are slow to get in and out of.

04 — Research

How people
really wait.

Before designing a seat, I watched how people rest when there isn't one.

Method 01

Observation

Watched how people wait in public spaces: leaning on walls, rails and railings.

Method 02

Posture and ergonomics

Studied half-standing postures to find where support is needed: lower back and seat.

Method 03

Benchmarking

Compared leaning rails, perch stools and other standing-support seating.

Key insights
  1. People already lean. The design should support that lean properly.
  2. Two contact points, back and seat, are enough for a short rest.
  3. It has to feel stable and be quick to step away from.
