03 — The Problem

Recycling only works when waste is sorted correctly. At most public bins people aren't sure what goes where, so it all ends up mixed.

How might we

…make recycling as simple as dropping something into one opening?

01

Confusing bins

Rows of bins with unclear labels slow people down.

02

Contaminated streams

One wrong item can spoil a whole bin of recyclables.

03

No feedback

People never see what happens to what they recycle.

04 — Research

Why good
intentions fail.

I looked at where public recycling breaks down, from the moment someone holds an empty bottle to the moment the bin is emptied.

Method 01

Observation

Watched how people use public bins and where they hesitate or give up.

Method 02

Interviews

Spoke with people and with staff who empty and sort the bins.

Method 03

Benchmarking

Compared reverse vending machines and smart bins on size, sorting and feedback.

Key insights
  1. If sorting takes more than a few seconds, people give up.
  2. The kiosk should do the sorting, not the user.
  3. Feedback on screen makes recycling feel worthwhile.
